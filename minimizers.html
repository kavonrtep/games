Long reads, sparse seeds

Nanopore and PacBio reads are thousands of bases long but contain 1–15 % errors, many of them insertions and deletions. Exact search of a whole read (Genome Index Lab) fails; instead a mapper samples short exact seeds, finds where many of them agree, and aligns only there.

This is how minimap2 (Li, 2018) works – the standard long-read mapper, also used for assembly-to-assembly comparison and spliced RNA reads.
