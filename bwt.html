The problem

A sequencer gives hundreds of millions of reads; each must be placed in a reference genome of 3 billion bases. Reading the whole genome for every read would take years. A read mapper therefore builds an index of the genome once, and then finds each read in time that hardly depends on the genome size.

The tabs go from the simplest index (a table of k-mers) to the one inside BWA and Bowtie (the FM-index, built on the Burrows–Wheeler transform). Minimizers, seed chaining and mapping quality follow in the next app.
