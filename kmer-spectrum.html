What this method does

Count every k-mer (word of length k) in the raw reads – no assembly, no reference – and plot how many different k-mers were seen once, twice, three times… The shape of this k-mer spectrum tells you:

  • the genome size (step 3),
  • the sequencing error rate (step 4),
  • the heterozygosity of a diploid genome (step 5),
  • how repetitive the genome is (step 6).

In the practical: jellyfish count + jellyfish histo, then GenomeScope. Every step here uses a genome and reads simulated in your browser, so the true answer is always known.
