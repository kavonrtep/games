Overlap – layout – consensus

  1. Overlap: compare every read with every other read; find where the end of one matches the beginning of another.
  2. Layout: arrange the reads using the overlaps – a path through the overlap graph.
  3. Consensus: stack the reads and take the majority base in every column; errors of single reads are outvoted.

OLC is how long reads (PacBio HiFi, Nanopore) are assembled – Canu, Hifiasm, Flye (a variant). Short reads are assembled with de Bruijn graphs instead: there are too many of them to compare all pairs. See the Assembly Explorer.

Overlap settings

Too short a minimum overlap accepts chance matches between unrelated reads; too long misses true overlaps. Allowing mismatches tolerates sequencing errors – and also slightly different repeat copies.
