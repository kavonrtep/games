How to read the plot

  • Sequence 1 runs left→right, Sequence 2 runs top→bottom. A dot at (x, y) means base x of Seq 1 equals base y of Seq 2.
  • Green lines parallel to the main diagonal: same-strand similarity (identity, direct repeats). A sideways jump of the diagonal = insertion/deletion.
  • Red lines perpendicular to the diagonal: the segment matches the reverse complement of Seq 2 (inversions, inverted repeats, palindromes). For short sequences the complement of Seq 2 is printed on the right axis – read it bottom→top.
  • Use Shuffle Seq 2 to see what chance alone produces with the current settings, and compare with the "expected by chance" statistic.
  • Click a cell to inspect the sequences around it; then use ← ↑ → ↓ to move, < > to slide along the diagonal and [ ] along the anti-diagonal.
